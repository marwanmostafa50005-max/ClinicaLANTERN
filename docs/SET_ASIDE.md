# Set-aside register

Seven criteria are out of the set in use after the audit and settlements of 19 November. Their
files stay in the repository so that the clinical reviewer can restore any of them. They are set
aside on licence, design, or Australian clinical-suitability grounds; this register does not call
the remaining criteria validated.

| Criterion | Reason it is set aside |
|---|---|
| EU7-F20 | As written it flags regimens consistent with Australian guidance, and its 2015 statements on reversal agents and rivaroxaban dosing are out of date. |
| EU7-F78 | Its rationale describes a bladder relaxant, while bethanechol makes the bladder contract; prostate enlargement alone does not establish bladder obstruction. |
| KHAWAGI-G005 | An age-only alert would flag sertraline or fluvoxamine treatment that Australian labels permit for children and adolescents. |
| KHAWAGI-G048 | Its only origins are the AGS Beers Criteria and a non-commercial paper, with no eligible independent basis for commercial use. |
| KHAWAGI-G157 | A fixed six-month carbamazepine level for every patient has no clinical basis, and its origin is non-commercial. |
| POPI-54 | The blanket oral-aciclovir omission prompt would flag supportive care recommended for most immunocompetent children and intravenous treatment recommended for immunocompromised children. |
| START-F6 | The Australian position statement found insufficient evidence to recommend routine probiotics for primary prevention of C. difficile infection with antibiotics. |

These seven were removed from `criteria/active.json` and the derived answer sheets in the final
database injection on 19 November (commit 872c1e1). Signed by the clinical reviewer: 0.
